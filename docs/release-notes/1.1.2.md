Capture 1.1.2: more FPS in your games, and full screen in the player.

- More FPS in games: with Game first (on the Quality page, on by default) the game uses the graphics card and the processor before Capture does. Turn it off if your clips stutter.
- Lighter on NVIDIA cards: the encoder leaves the graphics cores to the game, with the same quality.
- Full screen in the player: the button beside the sound, F, or a double-click on the clip. Esc leaves it.
- Updates are checked for even with instant replay off.
